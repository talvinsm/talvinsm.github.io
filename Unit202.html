Unit 20 Enterprise in IT - Assignment 2

Summary

As part of my BTEC Level 3 National Diploma in IT, I completed Unit 20: Enterprise in IT, a module focused on developing entrepreneurial and business-oriented skills within the technology sector. This is a summary of the unit's assignment 2, which required me to conceptualise, plan, and present a fully developed IT-based enterprise idea from the ground up.

The Project:

My chosen concept was a software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform designed to solve a real-world problem through innovative digital solutions. To protect the originality of the idea, I won’t disclose the app’s core features or functionality. However, the project involved comprehensive research, market validation, and business planning to ensure the product was both commercially viable and technically realistic.

Presentation and Communication:

As part of my assessment, I delivered a formal presentation showcasing my research findings, marketing strategy, and business proposal. This experience significantly improved my ability to communicate complex business concepts and demonstrate confidence when pitching ideas to an audience — simulating a real-world investor pitch scenario.

Outcomes

Reflection:

Completing this assignment gave me valuable insight into how technical innovation, business planning, and marketing strategy come together to build a successful IT product. It strengthened my entrepreneurial mindset and prepared me to approach future projects — and the tech industry — with both creative vision and commercial awareness.

Skills Developed:

Throughout this project, I developed a broad range of skills that blend IT and enterprise thinking:
  • Entrepreneurial and Strategic Thinking: Applying business models like SOSTAC and Ansoff to guide decision-making.

  • Market Research and Analysis: Gathering and interpreting data to identify opportunities and assess competition.

  • Marketing and Business Planning: Building a comprehensive marketing mix strategy tailored to a digital product.

  • Presentation and Communication: Delivering structured, persuasive presentations with confidence and clarity.

  • IT and Business Integration: Aligning a digital solution with realistic business models and customer needs.

  • Project Management and Critical Thinking: Managing deadlines, evaluating risks, and refining strategies based on feedback.

Tools Used

    Research and Strategic Planning:

    SOSTAC Framework: I structured my marketing plan using the SOSTAC model (Situation, Objectives, Strategy, Tactics, Action, and Control). This ensured a logical flow from market analysis to implementation and performance tracking.

    Ansoff Matrix: I applied the Ansoff Matrix to evaluate potential growth strategies. By analysing market penetration, product development, market development, and diversification options, I identified the most sustainable and low-risk growth path for my enterprise idea.

    Marketing Mix (4Ps): I developed a Marketing Mix strategy focusing on:
  • Product: Designing a digital product that delivers value, reliability, and usability to its intended audience.
  • Price: Establishing a pricing model aligned with customer expectations and competitor positioning.
  • Place: Planning effective digital distribution channels suitable for a SaaS environment.
  • Promotion: Outlining promotional strategies that build awareness and engagement within the target market.
